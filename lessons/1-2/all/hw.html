ARead six comments from people about AI and jobs. Who is writing each comment?

1The AI didn’t know our best customerRetold from a Reddit comment

A friend of mine was an assistant at a big firm. Her manager decided AI could do her job, so she was let go — not even a goodbye party. Guess who they had to call a few weeks later? Her. The AI didn’t know the phone number of the firm’s most loyal customer.

2My knowledge is expensive nowRetold from a Reddit comment

My friend was laid off for the same reason. When his old boss called and asked for help, he said: “Sure, but my knowledge is going to be expensive.” They took him back as a consultant — for $300 an hour.

3My manager loved AI more than designRetold from a Reddit comment

I’m a graphic designer and I lost my job ‘indirectly’ because of AI. My manager wasn’t a designer and didn’t understand how anything worked, but she was amazed by what AI could do. Every single day she asked me why I hadn’t done my task with AI.

4I trained my own replacementRetold from a Reddit comment

I worked as a game developer at a huge company. I had no idea they were using my own work to train the AI that would replace me. I was laid off three days before Christmas.

5Nobody understands the old codeWritten for this course in the same style

As a team lead, I was told to cut two junior developers because “AI writes the code now”. Six months later, nobody on the team understands our legacy system, and we’re paying a contractor twice their salary to fix it. Great savings.

6I just kept shouting “human”Written for this course in the same style

I spent forty minutes with my bank’s voice bot. It kept sending me back to the same menu. In the end I just kept shouting “Human! Agent!” until someone finally picked up. If a company replaces people with this, I’m moving my money.
