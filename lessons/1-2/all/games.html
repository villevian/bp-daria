Role-play

5Stay interview. A great developer has a job offer from a rival. You are the manager and want to keep her. Your teacher is the developer and has a secret reason for leaving — find it with good questions!

Manager (you): budget for a 10% pay rise OR a learning budget OR a 4-week sabbatical (only one!). Find out what she really wants before you offer anything.

Good questions: What would make you stay? · What will you be doing there that you can’t do here? · Where do you see yourself in two years? · What would you need to have achieved by next year to feel happy here?
🔒 Teacher only: the developer’s secretChoose one: (a) she is tired of menial tasks and wants AI work; (b) she wants a sabbatical to travel; (c) she is afraid of layoffs (“job hugging” the wrong way); (d) her manager never talks about promotion.

What did you offer? Did she stay?

Role-play

6The rehire call. Six months ago your company let an expert go (“AI can do this”). Now nobody understands the legacy system. Call her and bring her back. She knows her knowledge is expensive now!

A · Head of Engineering (you)
Max budget: $150/hour or a full-time job at the old salary +10%. Admit the mistake, but don’t lose face. Try to agree on a start date.
B · The ex-employee (teacher)
Wants $300/hour, remote, and a written apology to the team. Will accept less if they offer something she cares about.

We made a mistake when we… · We’d like to bring you back as… · What would it take…? · Let’s meet halfway. · Could we agree on a three-month contract?
